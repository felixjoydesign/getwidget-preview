    What is a LINE chat button?

    A LINE chat button is a floating button that opens a chat with your LINE Official Account. Visitors see a welcome message, tap once, and continue in LINE, the messenger they use every day.

    GetWidget builds it from your LINE Official Account ID. Enter the ID that starts with @, set your name, photo and greeting, then paste one embed snippet.
