FAQ

LINE Widget FAQ

Common questions about our LINE chat widget

How do I connect my LINE account?

Enter your LINE Official Account ID (the one that starts with @). The button opens a chat with that account.

Can I customize the chat bubble?

Yes. Set your display name, avatar, status, welcome message and button text, and place the button at the bottom left or bottom right.

Does it work with LINE Official Accounts?

Yes. The widget is built for LINE Official Accounts, which is what most businesses use.

Can multiple team members handle chats?

LINE Official Accounts let you add several admins in LINE's own tools. The widget simply opens the chat with your account.

What happens when a visitor clicks the button?

On mobile it opens the LINE app. On desktop, LINE shows a QR code to scan or opens the LINE desktop app.

Can I add a QR code for LINE?

The widget links directly to your LINE account. For in-store use, you can generate a QR code from your LINE Official Account dashboard.
