Website widget FAQ

Short answers to what people ask most before they sign up. Last updated October 2026.

What is a website widget?

A website widget is a ready-made feature, such as a review carousel, chat button or cookie banner, that you add to a page with a short embed code instead of building it yourself.

Is GetWidget free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 2 widgets and 250 views a month, with a GetWidget watermark. Paid plans start at $4/mo billed yearly or $5 billed monthly. Compare plans

How do I add a widget to my website?

Build your widget in the dashboard, click Install and copy the two snippets: a script tag and a div. Paste them into your site's HTML, or into a Custom HTML block in your site builder, and the widget appears. Step-by-step platform guides

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You design widgets in a visual editor and paste the embed code into your site. If your site builder lets you add custom HTML, you can install GetWidget.

Does it work with my website builder?

Yes. GetWidget works on WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, BigCommerce and any other site where you can paste HTML. You don't need to install a plugin or app. See the WordPress guide

Will GetWidget slow down my site?

The script loads asynchronously, so it does not block your page from rendering. Widgets appear once your content has loaded.

Can I use more than one widget on a page?

Yes. Each widget has its own embed code. Add as many widgets as your plan allows.

Do Google Reviews update automatically?

Not at the moment. Click Fetch Reviews in the builder whenever you want to pull in your latest reviews. About the Google Reviews widget

Does the Instagram feed update by itself?

Yes, on paid plans. It refreshes every 24 hours on Lite, every 12 hours on Standard, every 3 hours on Plus and every hour on Unlimited. On the Free plan the feed keeps the posts from when you set it up. About the Instagram feed widget

Can I remove the GetWidget watermark?

Yes. The watermark is removed on the Standard, Plus and Unlimited plans. See which plans remove it

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your dashboard.
