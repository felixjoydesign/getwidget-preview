FAQ

Google Review Widget FAQ

Everything about our Google Review widget

How do I connect my Google Business Profile?

Paste the Google Maps link for your business and click Fetch Reviews. No Google sign-in is needed.

Do reviews update automatically?

Not at the moment. Click Fetch Reviews in the builder whenever you want to pull in your latest reviews.

Can I choose which reviews appear?

You can sort by most relevant or highest rated, and choose how many reviews to show, up to 9.

How are the reviews displayed?

As a grid, slider, list or masonry layout.

Does the widget show the reviewer's name and photo?

Yes. Each review shows the reviewer's Google name and profile photo, just as they appear on Google.

What if I have multiple Google Business locations?

Create a separate widget for each location and embed it on the matching page. The Free plan includes one widget per type, so extra locations need a paid plan.
