      What is a Logo showcase widget?

      A logo showcase is a row or grid of client, partner or press logos on your website. It tells visitors at a glance that people they recognize already trust you.

      GetWidget builds it from logos you upload. Choose a scrolling ticker or a grid, set the size and colors, and embed it with one snippet. No design tools needed.

      How to add it

      How to add a logo showcase in three steps

      A couple of minutes from logo files to a live logo wall. No plugin and no code beyond one snippet.

      1. 01

        Add your client, partner or press logos as PNG, JPG, GIF or SVG files. SVG gives the sharpest result.

      2. 02

        Choose a scrolling ticker or a static grid, then set the logo size, grayscale, background and scroll speed.

      3. 03

        Copy the script and the div into your site once. Add or remove logos later and your page picks up the change.
