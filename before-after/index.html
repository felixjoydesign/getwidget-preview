    What is a Before and after slider?

    A before and after slider shows two images in the same frame, split by a divider. Visitors drag the divider to reveal more of one image or the other, so the change is obvious at a glance.

    GetWidget builds it from two images you upload or link. Set the labels, the divider and the starting position in a visual editor, then add one embed code to your site.

    01 · Images
    Upload JPG, PNG or GIF, or paste an image URL
    02 · Interaction
    Drag, click or tap. Works with touch
    03 · Several pairs
    Show them as a list or a carousel
    04 · Styling
    Labels, divider color and width, starting position

    How to add it

    How to add a before and after slider in three steps

    A couple of minutes from two photos to a live comparison. No plugin and no image editing.

    1. 01

      Upload a before and an after image, or paste their URLs. Use two images of the same size for a clean match.

    2. 02

      Write your labels, choose the starting position, the divider color and width, and add more pairs if you have them.

    3. 03

      Copy the script and the div into your site once. Edit the widget later and your page picks up the change.
