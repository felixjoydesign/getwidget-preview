FAQ

Before-After Slider Widget FAQ

Everything you need to know about our before-after slider

What image formats are supported?

Upload JPG, PNG or GIF images, or paste an image URL.

Can I customize the slider design?

Yes. Set the divider color and width, the starting position, and the label size and background, and choose whether to show the arrows.

Is the widget mobile-friendly?

Yes. It is responsive and supports touch, so visitors can drag the slider on phones and tablets.

Can I add multiple before-after comparisons?

Yes. Add several image pairs to one widget and show them as a list or a carousel.

Can I add labels to the before and after sides?

Yes. Add your own text labels to each side, such as 'Before' and 'After' or anything that fits.

What is the recommended image size?

Use two images with the same dimensions, at least 800px wide, for a sharp result on every device.
