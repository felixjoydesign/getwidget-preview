FAQ

Announcement Widget FAQ

Everything you need to know about our announcement bar

How do I create an announcement?

Write your message in the builder, add an optional button and pick your colors and position.

Can I schedule announcements?

Scheduling is not built in. Publish or unpublish the widget from your dashboard when a promotion starts or ends.

Can I customize the appearance?

Yes. Set the background and button colors, add a badge or image, and place the bar at the top or bottom of the screen or inline in your page.

How do visitors interact with announcements?

Add a button that links to any page, and optionally let visitors close the bar. A closed bar stays hidden for the rest of their visit.
