      What is a Website translation widget?

      A translation widget is a language switcher that translates your website in the visitor's browser. Visitors pick a language from a small dropdown and the text on the page changes right away, without a reload.

      GetWidget's switcher uses Google Translate. You choose the languages, the default language and where the dropdown sits. Your original pages stay exactly as they are.

      01 · Translation
      Google Translate, in the visitor's browser
      02 · Languages

      03 · Switcher
      A dropdown floating in a corner, or inline in your page
      04 · Reload
      None. The page translates in place
