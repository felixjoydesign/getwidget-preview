FAQ

Translation Widget FAQ

Everything you need to know about our translation widget

How many languages are supported?

Pick up to 10 languages per widget from a list of 15 widely spoken languages.

Is the translation automatic?

Visitors choose a language from the switcher and the page translates instantly. The widget does not switch languages based on the visitor's browser.

Can I customize which languages to show?

Yes. Choose the languages to offer and set a default language for your website.

How accurate are the translations?

Translations come from Google Translate. Machine translation is not perfect, but it works well for most website content.

Does it affect my website's SEO?

The widget translates content in the visitor's browser, so your original content stays unchanged for search engines.

Can I customize the language selector style?

The selector is a dropdown that floats in a corner of the screen or sits inline in your page. You can show flags, pick one of three sizes, and set the colors and corner radius.

What does it translate?

Text on the page when it loads. Text inside images, image alt text and form placeholders stay in the original language, and visitors pick their language again on each page.
