Positions

Six places for your popups, one click apart

Keep popups clear of your chat button or cookie banner. On phones they move to the top or bottom center.

  • Top left

    Near your logo and navigation. Seen early, out of the reading path.

  • Top center

    Front and center under the header, for announcements that matter.

  • Top right

    Next to the cart and account links, where shoppers look often.

  • Bottom left

    The classic spot. Leaves the right corner free for a chat button.

  • Bottom center

    Centered at the bottom edge, the same spot phones use.

  • Bottom right

    Where the eye finishes a page. Great for a final nudge.

What is a Notification popup widget?

A notification popup is a small message that slides in at the edge of a website. It points visitors to something worth their time, like a discount, a new product or an event, without covering the page.

With GetWidget you write every message yourself. Popups show what you type in the builder. They do not connect to your store or sign-up data, so nothing is made up.
