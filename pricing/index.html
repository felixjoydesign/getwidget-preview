Frequently asked questions

What does the Free plan include?

The Free plan includes 2 published widgets with 1 widget per type and 250 views per month. Great for trying out GetWidget.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes, you can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

What's the difference between plans?

Plans differ in published widget limits, monthly views, the watermark, and how often Instagram feeds refresh. Paid plans remove per-type restrictions.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. Every paid plan comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee.
