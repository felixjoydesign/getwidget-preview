FAQ

Cookie Banner FAQ

Everything you need to know about our cookie banner

Does this make my site GDPR compliant?

No banner can do that on its own. The widget shows the notice and buttons, but it does not block scripts or keep a record of consent. Check what your site needs with a privacy advisor.

Can I customize the banner position?

Yes. Show it as a bar at the top or bottom, or as a floating box at the bottom left, center or right.

What modes are supported?

Inform-only mode shows a notice with an accept button. Opt-in mode adds a decline button.

Can I link to my privacy policy?

Yes. Add a link to your privacy or cookie policy inside the banner.

Does the banner remember visitors?

After a visitor clicks a button, the banner stays hidden for the rest of their browser session. It shows again on their next visit.

Can I customize the banner text and buttons?

Yes. Edit the message and button text, and set the background color, button color and corner radius.
