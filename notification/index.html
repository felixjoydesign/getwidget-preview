FAQ

Notification Widget FAQ

Common questions about popup notifications

What kind of notifications can I show?

Any short message you write: a sale, a new product, an event or a blog post. Each popup can include an image, a link, a button and a badge.

Do notifications show real customer activity?

No. Popups show the messages you write in the builder. They do not connect to your store or sign-up data.

Can I customize how notifications look?

Yes. Pick a position (any corner, or top or bottom center), set the colors and corner radius, and choose the delay between popups.

How do I add notifications to my website?

Click Install in the builder and paste the embed code into your website. It works with any site builder or CMS.

Will notifications slow down my site?

The script loads asynchronously, so it does not block your page from rendering.

Do notifications appear on mobile?

Yes. On phones they appear at the top or bottom center of the screen, and visitors can swipe them away.
