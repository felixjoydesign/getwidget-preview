      What is a FAQ widget?

      An FAQ widget shows your frequently asked questions and their answers on your own website. Visitors find answers on their own, so fewer of the same questions land in your inbox.

      GetWidget builds the section in a visual editor. Add questions by hand or from a Google Sheet. No plugin and no code beyond one embed snippet.
