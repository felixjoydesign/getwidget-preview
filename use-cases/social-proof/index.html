What is social proof?

Social proof is the psychological principle that people follow the actions of others. When visitors see that other customers trust your business, they are more likely to take action themselves. On websites, social proof takes many forms: customer reviews, testimonials, social media posts and client logos.

Why it matters for your website

Before buying, most people look for what other customers say. Reviews, testimonials and recognizable client logos answer that question right on your page, instead of sending visitors off to search for it. Without social proof, your website is asking visitors to take your word for it.
